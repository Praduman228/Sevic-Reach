## 2. The problem Shegruh solves (core thesis)

Founder thesis [FB]: many interior and architecture firms have design talent and enough demand but stall because the business runs on the owner's memory, WhatsApp threads and personal spreadsheets. They have no standard way to quote, plan, track, bill or hand over a project. Taking on more projects therefore means more chaos, not more revenue. **Shegruh is the process layer**: one workflow that every project follows, with AI doing the repetitive paperwork.

What "no process" looks like day to day (use these to form hypotheses and ask questions; **never tell a prospect they have no process**):

- The owner approves every quote, chases every payment and answers every client message.
- Quotations live in Excel or Word and take days, so leads go cold.
- Site photos, client decisions and drawing changes are scattered across WhatsApp groups.
- Task ownership is unclear, there is no standard timeline, and the same mistakes repeat on each project.
- Cash-flow surprises: stages not billed on time, late payments, material overruns.
- Nobody knows how many more projects the team can take on.

## 4. How Shegruh works: the built-in process

Source: PRD §11 user journey [P], consistent with F §4–6. Every step feeds the next with no re-entry [F].

1. **Capture the lead** with project type, scope and budget.
2. **Log calls and follow-ups** against the lead.
3. **Measure the site** and attach measurements and images to the lead.
4. **Quotation / BOQ** generated by SheAI from images and a short brief.
5. **Proposal** shared with the client through a secure link.
6. **Client approves** and the lead converts to a project.
7. **Plan**: assign the team, create epics and tasks, and generate a timeline with SheAI.
8. **Execute**: tracker, daily site reports, material POs and receipts.
9. **Accounts**: income and expenses, invoices, payment requests to the client.
10. **Review** on the owner dashboard: leads, overdue tasks, finances, team workload.

## 14. Glossary

- **SheAI**: Shegruh's AI engine (chat/voice assistant and document generation).
- **BOQ / BOM**: bill of quantities / bill of materials. The product docs say BOM; Indian prospects and the Shegruh blog say BOQ. Use **BOQ** in India-facing copy.
- **SOP**: standard operating procedure; used internally. Avoid in prospect-facing messages ("a standard way of working" reads better).
- **Pilot**: 14-day guided trial on one live project (proposed in G).
- **Activation**: within 72 hours of signup the firm has (1) created or imported a live project, (2) generated a quotation, proposal or invoice, and (3) invited one colleague or one client [G].
- **GST**: India's goods and services tax; mention only for Indian prospects.
