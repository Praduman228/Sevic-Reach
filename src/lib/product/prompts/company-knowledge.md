# Shegruh features

Shegruh is an AI-powered practice management and site execution platform for interior designers, architects and design-build firms. SheAI is its AI assistant. The capabilities below are product facts, not promises of guaranteed accuracy, savings or legal protection.

- C1: One platform for leads, quotations, projects, site reports, accounts, materials and team management.
- C2: Lead pipeline, call/meeting/site-visit logs, follow-up reminders and embeddable inquiry forms capture location, budget and project type. Convert won leads into projects with measurements, notes and quotes carried over.
- C3: SheAI drafts editable space-wise quotations and trade-wise BOQ line items/specifications from scope, reference images, site photos or renders. Apply margins, GST and discounts; reuse studio rates/templates and export themed, branded PDFs with terms and bank details.
- C4: SheAI generates project schedules from scope, budget, duration and lead data, and rebalances existing timelines after delays or approval holdups.
- C5: Visual AI drafts editable BOM/BOQ estimates from renders or inspiration photos: furniture, cabinetry, finishes, hardware, dimensions, quantities, labour and installation. Budget, Premium and Luxury tiers; city/state-based cost calibration and brand recommendations; save to projects or export PDFs. Estimates require team review.
- C6: Mobile daily site reports capture trade-wise manpower, executed work, deliveries/challans, quality checks, obstacles, visits/instructions, tomorrow's plan and time-stamped photos; export client-ready PDF reports.
- C7: Interactive Gantt with phases, tasks, durations, dependencies and milestones; Kanban/list views with bulk editing, assignees, priorities, subtasks, attachments and discussions. Cross-project master planner shows resource bottlenecks.
- C8: Project income/expense ledger with bills and payment records; contract value, invoiced/received amounts, expenses and margin visibility. GST invoices with sequencing, GSTIN/PAN, milestone billing, mark-as-paid ledger updates and branded PDFs with bank details/UPI QR.
- C9: Vendor-linked purchase orders and delivery tracking; goods-received checks with challans/photos; material catalog, units, site stock, threshold alerts and room/task-linked consumption deductions, linked to accounting.
- C10: Owner dashboard for leads, overdue work and finances across projects.
- C11: Staff directory with designations/departments, role-based access, web/mobile attendance, leave requests/approvals/accrual and availability calendar; workload/capacity and task-velocity views.
- C12: Proposals and quotations shared for client approval through a link.
- C13: SheAI chat is available across the workspace: answers project/task/business/financial questions, creates leads/tasks/projects and follow-ups, drafts BOQs/documents, and analyses site images/renders for materials and estimated dimensions.
- C14: AI client brief refiner turns rough notes or bullet points into editable professional design briefs.
- C15: AI design review audits uploaded plans, elevations, electrical layouts and renders for clearances, door swings, ergonomics, lighting/material compatibility, joinery and potential execution clashes. Findings support professional review, not certified compliance.
- C16: AI proposal generator uses requirements, moodboards and scope to draft vision, objectives, deliverables, timeline/milestones and commercial/payment stages; reorder sections, edit rich text and export branded PDFs.
- C17: Editable document/contract generation fills studio, client, site and payment details into design agreements, NDAs, LOIs, project briefs, consultant/contractor agreements, liability waivers, permit checklists, change orders, BOQs, handovers and completion certificates; preview, print/PDF export. Not a substitute for legal advice.
- C18: Private client portal with mobile OTP access, progress/milestone dashboard, simplified task tracker, read-only Gantt, invoices/payment ledger, daily site photos/notes and approved documents; installable mobile PWA.
- C19: Cloud file vault with lead/project folders for drawings, renders, photos, bills and agreements; CAD/DWG, PDF, images, videos, spreadsheets and documents; per-file/folder client visibility and secure previews/downloads.
- C20: Personal workspace with priority/deadline to-dos linked to leads/projects, unified meetings/site-visits calendar, private concepts/notes/files and productivity insights.
- C21: Categorised vendor/contractor directory for civil, materials, finishing, MEP, specialist trades and equipment; profiles hold contacts, GSTIN and address, linked to POs, deliveries and expense vouchers.
- C22: Studio identity, logo, address, registration details and website; branded letterhead across quotations, proposals, reports, agreements and invoices; scalable team seats, active projects and storage. Do not invent plan limits or pricing.
